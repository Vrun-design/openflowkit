# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Builders, software architects, and engineering teams working under delivery pressure. They need to express, inspect, and revise technical systems through direct manipulation, concise text, or agents without translating between incompatible tools.

## Product Purpose

OpenFlowKit is a free, local-first, agent-native infinite canvas for technical diagrams. Success means canvas interaction feels immediate, text and canvas round-trip deterministically, and agents can perform every meaningful human action against the same live document model.

## Brand Personality

Precise, fast, and quietly confident. The product should feel like a trusted engineering instrument: approachable without being playful, powerful without becoming visually loud.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI dashboards, decorative glass, ornamental gradients, oversized rounding, modal-heavy workflows, and diagram editors whose connectors fight the user. Do not imitate Koboyo's surface; exceed its text pipeline while keeping FigJam/tldraw-class direct manipulation.

## Design Principles

1. Canvas first: editing surface keeps priority over chrome.
2. One model, many inputs: canvas, DSL, imports, AI, and MCP converge on one deterministic pipeline.
3. Immediate by default: pointer, keyboard, and text actions never wait on decoration or heavy main-thread work.
4. Progressive power: common actions stay obvious; advanced controls appear in context.
5. Honest and recoverable: diagnostics explain loss, destructive regeneration is explicit, and each user intent is one undo step.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for product chrome. Every action has a keyboard path, focus remains visible, state never relies on color alone, touch targets remain usable, semantic scene structure supports assistive technology, and reduced-motion preferences remove nonessential movement.
